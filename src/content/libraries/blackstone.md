I biked down here (going over an awesome [pedestrian bridge](https://architizer.com/projects/43rd-street-bridge-chicago/)) on a really nice day.

It was a bit of a hike but a really pretty building modelled after the Greek Acropolis.

It was pretty small - mostly one floor with some fiction on a second floor (with a cool glass floor).

While it took over an hour to get down here, definitely was a cool spot, and a nice way to get a big bike ride in.
