After visiting [Rogers Park](/libraries/northtown), I made my way over to Budlong Woods.

I was pretty hungry by this point, so I grabbed some fried codfish at [a nearby seafood shop](https://mycrab.us/).

Budlong Woods was named after a prominent local pickle farmer. Which I did not know that Chicago could have prominent local pickle farmers. Budlong had a fairly large Korean language section, which I spotted an Otessa Mosfegh translation from where I was studying.

Afterwards, I headed to [Sulzer Regional](/libraries/sulzer_regional). It was my first time going by the North Shore Channel Trail.
