I biked up here mostly on the Lakefront Trail. The library was a really cozy spot. It was small but pretty nice. I was sitting next to a large film collection and noticed a book by [Kasparov about AI](https://www.kasparov.com/deep-thinking-ai/) on the shelf, which was interesting while studying for Reinforcement Learning class.

I met up with a buddy who lives nearby and we grabbed some great [Chinese food for lunch for lunch](https://yooyeechicago.com/).

"Bezazian" is named after a [WWII veteran and aspiring writer](https://www.chipublib.org/about-bezazian-branch/). Interestingly, neither this branch nor Harold Bezazian seem to have a Wikipedia page.
