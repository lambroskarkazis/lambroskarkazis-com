Rogers Park was a nice bike ride. Up further north than I have been in the city before. I went up the Lakefront Trail, then through Loyola's campus. They were doing some kind of new student enrollment and it was a beautiful day.

I got up to Roger's park and it had a nice upstairs reading room. Good power outlet coverage.
