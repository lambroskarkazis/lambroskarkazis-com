I'd never been here, but this library was quite close. Walked there. Returned a (slightly overdue) book I'd checked out from [Harold Washington](/libraries/harold_washington_library) about a month ago.

Classic "children's section downstairs - adults upstairs" setup. Plenty of desk space. Interesting view mostly of taller apartment buildings in the area (kinda similar to [Edgewater](/libraries/edgewater)).

Got a decent amount done here and walked home.
