Wicker Park Bucktown was my old homebase when I lived in Pulaski Park.

It is right across the street from [Royal Palms Shuffleboard](https://www.royalpalmschicago.com/) and a block south of the [606 trail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomingdale_Trail).

It is a nice spot. The reading room is quiet and a really nice place to study. Good access to outlets and fun part of town to be in.
