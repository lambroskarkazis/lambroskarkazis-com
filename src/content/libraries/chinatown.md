I really enjoyed the Chinatown public library. It is right off of the Red Line. There are alot of great restraurants and shops to walk around to take a break. I came down and ate lunch, worked for a while, then Christine joined me for dinner.

They had a huge Chinese language selection.

The library was not as big inside as it looked.
