The Harold Washington Library is incredible. It is very accessible with its [own CTA stop](https://www.transitchicago.com/station/hwlc/) for the Loop "L" trains.

The library has 9 floors with the top being the pictured Winter Garden. This is a good library to work at as there is alot of seating and outlets. It has a great selection of material if you want to check something out while you're there.
