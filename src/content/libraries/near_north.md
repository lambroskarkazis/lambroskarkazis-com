Part of the goal with this project has been to explore more areas of the city. Near North is interesting, since I used to see it twice a day from the Brown Line during my commute.

However, I had never visited, so it was interesting to go and see it.

It was just a single floor. It had decent seating. Decent natural light from the ceiling and windows. I was tempted by the magazine/newspaper section but was a bit behind on my paper.

I walked home afterwards and was interested to see some new stuff being a few blocks off where I usually walk.
