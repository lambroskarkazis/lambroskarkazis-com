Logan Square was a nice experience. Biking up Milwaukee with the protected bike lanes was fun. Coming up to the library, they had a nice outdoor seating / bench area. Coming in, there was a large childrens' section with a ton of parents and kids. Walked by a guy playing chess on one of the computers. There was a skylight so it was really bright in there.

Grabbed a slice of pizza on the corner of the block after I left.
