I went up here with my sister. A man on the Red line told us about a [coyote that swam to Alcatraz](https://www.nps.gov/goga/learn/news/alcatraz-coyote-wasn-t-a-city-boy-after-all.htm/index.htm) since I had an Alcatraz shirt on. They had a nice room on the second floor with plenty of seating and outlets. Good views of the surrounding buildings. We got [Latin food](https://myparrilla.com/) after we left.
