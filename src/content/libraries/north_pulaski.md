Had an interesting ride up here. Stopped at the microcenter to redeem a coupon for a free USB flash drive. Got to bike on the [606 trail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomingdale_Trail) after that. Got further than I ever had before on North Avenue.

I did not have super high hopes coming up, as this library was in a strip mall. I also was coming in very sweaty since it was very hot outside.

However, the staff were very nice. It was small but I had a seat to study in. The librarians were very patient with multiple patrons trying to print their resumes (recession indicator?).

I had aspirations for another library crawl starting from here. However, it was extremely hot, and a thunderstorm was forecast for the afternoon. So I biked back down the 606 and came home.
