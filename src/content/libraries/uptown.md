After visiting [Bezazian](/libraries/bezazian) and getting lunch with a friend, I hit Uptown library on the way home. This was by a [massive cemetary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graceland_Cemetery).

This library was pretty crowded and seemed like it may have been an old school. There were a bunch of origami swans hanging from the ceiling on the way in. There was a nice skylight in the center. The library was pretty crowded, and one of the patrons was playing a CS:GO clone on a library computer.
