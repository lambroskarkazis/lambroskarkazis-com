Lincoln Park is my home base / closest library. Great spot right near the Depaul Campus. Walkable for me so a go-to if I am not sure where I want to go or do not want to stray too far.

This library is close to the Fullerton Brown/Red Line Stop, which is where I'll take to many of the other libaries.

Generally - pretty good outlet access, decent natural light. Grateful to have it pretty close.
