After [Budlong Woods](/libraries/budlong_woods), I made my last stop of the day at Sulzer Regional.

I knew that [Harold Washington Library](/libraries/harold_washington_library) was the main library for CPL. However, I did not realize that there were "regional" libraries (vs the normal "neighborhood" libraries). [Sulzer](https://www.chipublib.org/locations/67), [Legler](https://www.chipublib.org/locations/42), and [Woodson](https://www.chipublib.org/locations/81) are the three "regional" branches in the CPL system. It is not completely clear to me what distinction those have for the regular patron.

I will say, Sulzer Regional was quite nice. It had the first floor with the children's section and bathrooms. The second floor had alot of seating. There appeared to be a little third floor like a balcony that I saw as I was exiting. There was a ton of windows and natural light. And you could see between the floors. There was a nice view to the park across the street.

This was my fourth library of the day, so I was fading a little. I biked down Lincoln trying to keep up with an e-biker on my way home.
